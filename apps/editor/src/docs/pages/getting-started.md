# Getting Started

Welcome to **Mythic Forge** — a lightweight engine for making games and interactive 3D scenes on your phone, laptop or PC.

## Your first scene in five minutes

1. On the Home screen, press **New Project**.
2. Give it a name, choose **3D Game**, your platform, and the **Balanced** profile.
3. Pick the **Third Person** template and press **Create Project**.
4. The editor opens. Press **Add → Cube** to place a cube in front of the camera.
5. Drag the coloured arrows (the *gizmo*) to move it. Switch to **Rotate** or **Scale** in the toolbar.
6. With the cube selected, open the **Inspector** and change its **Material → Colour**.
7. Press **Play** (▶). Move with **WASD** or the on-screen stick, jump with **Space** or **Jump**.
8. Press **Stop** (■). The scene returns exactly to how it was before you played.
9. Press **Save**.

## Try the demo

**Home → Explore → Demo: Shrine of Lamps** creates a small finished game made with official Mythic Bharat Studios assets. Open it, press Play, and walk to every diya.

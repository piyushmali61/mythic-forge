# Privacy & Your Data

**Mythic Forge 0.1 has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no account.** It does not send information about you or your projects anywhere.

## What stays on your device
- Projects, imported assets and backups
- Downloaded library assets and cached thumbnails
- Recovery snapshots of unsaved work
- Settings

Everything is stored in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) removes it — export projects you want to keep.

## When the network is used
- When you ask to check an online asset repository (only if one is configured for your build)
- When you open an external link (for example an asset's source page)

Turn all network access off in **Settings → Network**. Everything bundled with the app keeps working offline.

## Your content
Projects you create are yours. Imported files stay private to your project unless you export it. Mythic Bharat Studios assets remain under their licence; third-party assets remain under their original licences.

## Diagnostics
**Settings → Privacy → Export logs** saves the app log to a file you control, for attaching to a bug report. Logs contain app events, not project content; user names in file paths and e-mail addresses are removed. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

## Erasing everything
**Settings → Privacy → Erase all data** permanently removes everything Mythic Forge stored on this device.

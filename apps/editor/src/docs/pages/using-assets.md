# Using the Asset Library

The library has two sections.

## Mythic Bharat Studios
Original assets owned by Mythic Bharat Studios — Indian architecture, props, nature and materials. You may use them in personal and commercial projects and modify them. You may not redistribute the asset files on their own. Read the full **Mythic Bharat Studios Asset License** from any asset page.

## Free & Open
Third-party assets whose licences allow them to be shared through Mythic Forge. An asset appears here only after its creator, source, licence and permissions have been recorded and checked. This section is empty until assets complete that review.

## Asset pages
Each asset shows its preview, description, creator, licence, source, format, size, triangle count, textures, animations, compatibility and — when required — a ready-to-copy **attribution**.

The licence panel shows **LICENCE VERIFIED** only when every check passed and a named reviewer signed it off. Development builds may also show assets marked **Pending review**; release builds hide them.

## Download and add
- **Download** stores the asset on this device (with an integrity check).
- **Add to project** copies it into a project. Its licence information goes with it, so exported projects and builds keep the credits.
- Projects never change silently: if a newer version is published, the project's Assets panel shows **Update** and asks before replacing anything.

## Credits
When you export a web build, required attributions are added to the game's **Credits** screen automatically. Check them before publishing.

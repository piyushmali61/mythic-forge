# Publishing

Before you share a game made with Mythic Forge:

1. **Check your rights.** You must own, or have permission for, everything you imported.
2. **Check credits.** Open your build's Credits screen. Every asset that requires attribution must be listed.
3. **Mythic Bharat Studios assets** may ship inside your game, but not as separate files anyone can extract and reuse as an asset pack.
4. **Test on real devices**, especially a low-end phone. Use the benchmark in Settings → Performance.
5. **Store rules change.** App stores (for example Google Play) update their technical and policy requirements regularly — target API level, app bundle format, signing, privacy policy, data-safety form, content rating, permissions, account deletion. Check the store's current official documentation at the time you publish. Mythic Forge does not guarantee compliance for you.
6. **Privacy.** If your game collects any data, you need a privacy policy that says what and why. Games built with Mythic Forge 0.1 collect nothing by themselves.

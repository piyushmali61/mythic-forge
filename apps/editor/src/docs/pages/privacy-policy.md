# Privacy Policy (draft)

> **Draft for Mythic Forge 0.1 — not yet reviewed by legal counsel.** It describes what this version of the app actually does. It must be reviewed, completed with the publisher's legal details, and updated whenever the app's behaviour changes.

**Publisher:** Mythic Bharat Studios · **Contact:** *to be added before release*

## Summary
Mythic Forge works on your device. This version does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.

## Information we collect
None. Mythic Forge 0.1 contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and has no user accounts.

## Information stored on your device
Your projects, imported files, downloaded library assets, cached previews, recovery snapshots and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot access them. You can delete them at any time (Settings → Privacy → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app).

## Network use
The app connects to the internet only when you ask it to — for example to check an online asset repository (if the build is configured with one) or to open an external link. Such requests are standard HTTPS requests; the receiving server may log technical information such as your IP address as part of normal operation.

## Permissions
The Android app requests no runtime permissions. It uses the system file picker and share sheet only when you choose files or export.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If a future version adds optional features that process personal data (such as accounts, cloud backup or opt-in analytics), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and they will be off until you choose to use them.
